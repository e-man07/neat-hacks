# Billing and plans

Lumen has three plans: Free, Growth and Enterprise.

## Plan limits

The Free plan includes 1 million events per month and 1 year of data retention. Growth costs $49 per month per 5 million events and keeps data for 3 years. Enterprise is priced annually and keeps data for 7 years.

## Overages

When a Growth workspace goes over its event allowance, Lumen keeps ingesting and bills overage at $8 per additional million events. Free workspaces are paused at the limit instead; ingestion resumes on the first day of the next month.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of the renewal date. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the 1st of each month. Download past invoices from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
