# Integrations

## Slack

The Slack integration posts a chart or dashboard to a channel on a schedule, daily or weekly. Alerts can also be sent to Slack when a metric moves more than a threshold you set.

## Segment

Lumen can be added as a Segment destination. Events, identifies and page calls are mapped automatically.

## Webhooks

Cohort webhooks call your URL when a user enters or leaves a cohort. Webhooks retry for 24 hours with exponential backoff.
