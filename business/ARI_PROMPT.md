# Building the plan in cfo.ai

Two models live in cfo.ai. Night Shift reads the first one at runtime (mirrored into
`business/unit_economics.yaml`); the second is the plan we submit.

## 1. Harbor Goods — support economics (what an incident costs)

Prompt for Ari (or for Claude Code with the cfo.ai MCP attached):

> Build a model called "Harbor Goods — support economics" for an online outdoor-gear store whose
> customer support is handled by an AI agent. Variables: support conversations per hour (day 480,
> night 90), customer lifetime value $260, probability a customer churns after a failed support
> conversation 8%, fully loaded cost of a human support ticket $4.50, chargeback fee $15, dispute rate
> on wrongly denied refunds 25%. Derive: cost of a failed conversation (churn × LTV = $20.80), cost of
> a missed refund, and cost per minute of an incident given a loss-per-conversation input. Add an
> on-call table: minutes until a human responds (business hours 15, evening 45, night 300). Add a
> scenario "Night incident" with loss per conversation $50 at night volume and show the projected
> loss if nobody acts until morning.

## 2. Night Shift — business plan (pricing, costs, revenue, runway)

> Build a 24-month plan for "Night Shift", a SaaS that acts as the on-call engineer for companies running
> customer-facing AI agents. Use the assumptions and measured unit costs in the table below (from
> business/PLAN.md, generated by business/model.py from our live benchmark). Pricing: Hobby $0, Team
> $399/month, Scale $1,990/month, 15% of paying customers on Scale. Costs: 2 founders at $4,500/month,
> first engineer from month 9 at $5,500/month, base infrastructure $400/month, $18 per customer per
> month infrastructure, LLM cost per customer from measured investigations, 3.5% payment fees,
> marketing $1,500/month from month 4. Starting cash $150,000. New paid customers ramp 0,0,1,2,3…
> to 22 per month, 3% monthly logo churn. Show MRR, costs, net burn, cash, runway and break-even month.
> Add scenarios: "Slow sales" (half the new customers) and "LLM prices double".

Then share each model as a read-only Page and put the link in `web/public/business.json`
(`cfo_share_url`) and in the README, and export the plan into `business/`.
